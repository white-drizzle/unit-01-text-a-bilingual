引言：通过互联互通与经济合作促进增长和可持续发展Introduction: Growth and Sustainable Development Through Connectivity and Economic Cooperation

第 1 段 Paragraph 1

EN

The world is facing a seemingly permanent slowdown in economic growth after the global financial crisis. Trade and investment growth has been on the decline for several years and it appears that the reasons for this development are rather structural than cyclical. Monetary and fiscal policies in the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) countries, as well as in emerging markets are facing severe limitations of effectiveness. Therefore, new ways of stimulating growth are high on the agenda. In order to foster investment in industry and services and increase employment opportunities, infrastructure investment on a large scale, as well as trade and investment agreements have become central to the economic policy debate. The scaling up of infrastructure investment is seen as a strategic pathway to lift global growth and foster employment creation and sustainable development.

中译

全球金融危机之后，世界经济增长似乎长期处于放缓状态。贸易和投资增长已经连续数年下降，造成这一变化的原因似乎更多是结构性的，而非周期性的。经济合作与发展组织（OECD）成员国以及新兴市场的货币和财政政策，其有效性正受到严重限制。因此，寻求刺激经济增长的新途径成为议程上的重要事项。为促进工业和服务业投资并增加就业机会，大规模基础设施投资以及贸易和投资协定已成为经济政策讨论的核心议题。扩大基础设施投资被视为提振全球增长、促进创造就业和可持续发展的一条战略路径。

词句讲解
  • a seemingly permanent slowdown：seemingly 为副词，表示“看似、似乎”；slowdown 指增长或活动的放缓。seemingly 提醒读者这是文中的判断，并不等于已经证明永久持续。
  • on the decline：on the decline 是固定搭配，表示“处于下降过程中”；与 decline 作动词的用法不同。
  • rather structural than cyclical：structural 指经济结构方面的，cyclical 指随经济周期起伏的；rather ... than ... 表示“与其说……不如说……”。
  • scaling up of infrastructure investment：scaling up 是 scale up 的动名词结构，指扩大规模；介词 of 引出扩大的是基础设施投资。

第 2 段 Paragraph 2

EN

Estimates of infrastructure “gaps” amount to trillions of dollars. Particularly in Asia, the need for infrastructure investments in energy, transport, water and communication is enormous. The Asian Development Bank (ADB) estimates the need for $26 trillion in infrastructure spending in Asia between 2016 and 2030.

中译

据估算，基础设施“缺口”达到数万亿美元。尤其是在亚洲，对能源、交通、水利和通信基础设施投资的需求十分巨大。亚洲开发银行（ADB）估计，2016年至2030年间，亚洲需要26万亿美元的基础设施支出。

词句讲解
  • amount to trillions of dollars：amount to 表示“总计达到”；trillions of dollars 为“数万亿美元”，不等同于数十亿美元。
  • the need for infrastructure investments：the need for 后接名词，表示“对……的需求”；infrastructure 指基础设施，investments 此处指投资。
  • between 2016 and 2030：between ... and ... 标明该笔需求估算覆盖的时间区间，并非单一年份的支出。

第 3 段 Paragraph 3

EN

China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) aims to enhance global connectivity and economic cooperation. It seeks to address the slowdown in economic growth by promoting infrastructure investment and trade agreements. The need for infrastructure investment, particularly in Asia, is significant, with estimates reaching trillions of dollars. The BRI is seen as a strategic vision to boost trade and investment flows, improve regional supply networks, reduce trade barriers and foster participation in value chains. The Initiative offers opportunities for countries to integrate into regional and global value chains, increase trade and attract Chinese foreign investment. However, challenges include the transition to sustainable development models, meeting the requirements of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the Paris Agreement and adopting low-carbon energy technologies. This overview focuses on the opportunities and challenges of the BRI, excluding foreign and security policy considerations, and touches on finance and institutional issues.

中译

中国的“一带一路”倡议旨在加强全球互联互通和经济合作。它通过推动基础设施投资和贸易协定，试图应对经济增长放缓。基础设施投资的需求相当大，亚洲尤其如此，估算金额达到数万亿美元。“一带一路”被视为一项战略构想，旨在促进贸易和投资流动、改善区域供应网络、减少贸易壁垒，并推动参与价值链。该倡议为各国融入区域和全球价值链、增加贸易以及吸引中国对外投资提供了机会。然而，面临的挑战包括向可持续发展模式转型、满足《2030年可持续发展议程》和《巴黎协定》的要求，以及采用低碳能源技术。本文概述重点讨论“一带一路”倡议的机遇与挑战，不涉及外交和安全政策方面的考量，并简要触及融资与制度问题。

词句讲解
  • aims to enhance global connectivity：aim to do 表示“旨在做……”；connectivity 在此指国家和地区之间基础设施、经贸等方面的互联互通。
  • with estimates reaching trillions of dollars：with + 名词 + 现在分词构成补充说明结构，表示“据估算，其金额达到数万亿美元”。
  • integrate into regional and global value chains：integrate into 表示“融入”；value chain 指从投入、生产到销售等环节构成的价值链。
  • excluding foreign and security policy considerations：excluding 是现在分词引出的范围说明，表示“不包括外交和安全政策方面的考量”；touches on 表示“简要涉及”。

中国的“一带一路”倡议China's Belt and Road Initiative

第 4 段 Paragraph 4

EN

China's Belt and Road Initiative, introduced in 2013, aims to connect regions through two routes: the historical Silk Road (land-based) and a maritime road. The Initiative is open to all countries interested in participating. It also seeks to develop underdeveloped areas in China by linking them to Central and South Asia. The estimated investment for the first phase is around $240 billion, subject to revision based on the number of participating countries and sectors covered. The BRI aims to create an open, inclusive and balanced regional economic cooperation architecture. It promotes economic policy coordination, facilities connectivity, unimpeded trade, financial integration and people-to-people bonds. Existing multilateral cooperation mechanisms like the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) and Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) are encouraged to strengthen communication and attract more participants. While there are limited identified projects outside China, notable ones include investments in Pakistan's energy sector and transport connections, as well as funding agreements with Indonesia and Egypt. However, many project ideas are still under consideration, indicating a lack of fully developed projects and clear business rationale. Collaboration with international and local companies is essential for the Initiative's success. Harmonizing standards for cross-border transport connections, such as railways, may pose a significant challenge. The BRI's concept, scope and nature remain fluid and will evolve over time based on the engagement of countries, investors and financiers.

中译

中国于2013年提出“一带一路”倡议，旨在通过两条路线连接不同地区：历史上的陆上丝绸之路和海上通道。该倡议向所有有意参与的国家开放。它还希望通过将中国欠发达地区与中亚和南亚连接起来，推动这些地区的发展。第一阶段的估计投资约为2,400亿美元，具体数额可能根据参与国家数量和所涵盖的行业而调整。“一带一路”倡议旨在构建开放、包容、均衡的区域经济合作架构。它推动经济政策协调、设施互联互通、贸易畅通、资金融通和民心相通。文章鼓励上海合作组织（SCO）、亚太经济合作组织（APEC）等现有多边合作机制加强沟通，吸引更多参与方。尽管中国境外已经明确的项目有限，值得注意的项目包括对巴基斯坦能源部门和交通连接的投资，以及与印度尼西亚和埃及达成的融资协议。不过，许多项目设想仍在考虑之中，这表明充分成熟的项目和清晰的商业依据仍然不足。与国际和当地企业合作，对这一倡议的成功至关重要。协调跨境交通连接（如铁路）的标准，可能带来重大挑战。“一带一路”的概念、范围和性质仍不固定，并将随着各国、投资者及融资方的参与而逐步演变。

词句讲解
  • introduced in 2013：过去分词短语置于逗号之间，补充说明前面的 Initiative，相当于 which was introduced in 2013。
  • subject to revision：subject to 表示“取决于、可能受到……影响”；subject to revision 意为“有待调整”，不是“服从复习”。
  • unimpeded trade：unimpeded 意为“不受阻碍的、畅通的”；unimpeded trade 指贸易畅通。
  • remain fluid and will evolve over time：remain 为系动词，fluid 在此指“尚不固定、仍有变化空间”；evolve over time 表示“随着时间逐渐演变”。

亚洲互联互通倡议：经验与教训Asian Connectivity Initiatives—Lessons Learned

第 5 段 Paragraph 5

EN

Existing cooperation and connectivity programs in Asia have yielded varied outcomes, with some regions showing advanced initiatives like the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), while others, particularly South Asia, continue to lag behind in developing trade and transport links.

中译

亚洲现有的合作和互联互通计划取得了不同程度的成果：一些地区的倡议进展较为领先，例如东南亚国家联盟（ASEAN）；而另一些地区，尤其是南亚，在发展贸易和交通联系方面仍然落后。

词句讲解
  • have yielded varied outcomes：yield 在此表示“产生、取得”；varied outcomes 指各不相同的结果或成果。
  • continue to lag behind：lag behind 表示“落后、进展迟缓”；continue to do 强调这种状态仍在延续。
  • with some regions showing：with + 名词 + 现在分词引出伴随情况；后面的 while 与其形成对比。

第 6 段 Paragraph 6

EN

Key findings from recent studies on connectivity and regional cooperation in Asia include overcoming complex barriers to economic integration, such as infrastructure limitations, financial markets, trade facilitation, trade barriers and limited regional cooperation, which can unlock significant growth potential. Emphasizing cooperation between South Asia and Southeast Asia showcases the economic benefits.

中译

近期关于亚洲互联互通和区域合作的研究的重要发现包括：克服经济一体化方面的复杂障碍，例如基础设施、金融市场、贸易便利化、贸易壁垒和区域合作不足等方面的问题，可以释放巨大的增长潜力。重视南亚与东南亚之间的合作，体现出这一合作的经济效益。

词句讲解
  • barriers to economic integration：barrier to 后接名词或动名词，表示“……的障碍”；economic integration 指经济一体化。
  • trade facilitation：指通过简化程序、改善通关与运输等方式促进贸易便利化，不等同于单纯扩大贸易规模。
  • unlock significant growth potential：unlock 在此为比喻用法，意为“释放”；growth potential 是“增长潜力”。

第 7 段 Paragraph 7

EN

Enhancing transport and energy connectivity is crucial for promoting economic integration. There is untapped potential for energy trade, necessitating the interconnection of electric power grids. Building effective institutions to facilitate cross-regional coordination and address coordination gaps is a major challenge, especially when policies need to be developed across multiple countries.

中译

加强交通和能源互联互通，对促进经济一体化至关重要。能源贸易仍有尚未开发的潜力，因此有必要实现电力网络的互联。建立有效的机构以促进跨区域协调并弥合协调缺口，是一项重大挑战；当多国需要共同制定政策时，情况尤其如此。

词句讲解
  • untapped potential：untapped 指“尚未开发或利用的”；potential 在此作名词，意为“潜力”。
  • necessitating the interconnection：necessitating 是现在分词，引出前述情况带来的必要结果；interconnection 指互相连接。
  • facilitate cross-regional coordination：facilitate 意为“促进、使更容易”；cross-regional coordination 指跨区域协调。

第 8 段 Paragraph 8

EN

Deep integration, focusing on trade barriers like Non-tariff Measures (NTMs), services, competition policy, intellectual property protection and sensitive goods such as agriculture, yields more substantial benefits than merely pursuing infrastructure projects.

中译

深层一体化着重解决非关税措施（NTMs）等贸易壁垒，以及服务业、竞争政策、知识产权保护和农业产品等敏感商品问题，其收益比仅仅推进基础设施项目更为显著。

词句讲解
  • Deep integration：在本文语境中指超越基础设施建设，进一步处理服务、竞争规则和知识产权等政策问题的深层一体化。
  • Non-tariff Measures (NTMs)：Non-tariff Measures 意为“非关税措施”，NTMs 是其缩写；原文将其作为需要关注的贸易壁垒示例。
  • more substantial benefits than merely pursuing：more ... than ... 表示比较；merely 意为“仅仅”，修饰 pursuing，突出与只推进基建项目的比较。

第 9 段 Paragraph 9

EN

Trade facilitation measures are critical for reducing trade-related costs and ensuring that the advantages of infrastructure investment translate into actual trade growth. It is vital to address issues beyond customs, such as port facilities, transport facilities, excessive documentation, immigration and quarantine requirements and information technology.

中译

贸易便利化措施对于降低贸易相关成本、确保基础设施投资的优势转化为实际贸易增长至关重要。解决海关之外的问题也十分重要，例如港口设施、运输设施、过多的单证手续、出入境和检疫要求，以及信息技术问题。

词句讲解
  • Trade facilitation measures：facilitation 意为“便利化、促进”；此处 measures 指具体措施。
  • translate into actual trade growth：translate into 在此不是“翻译成”，而是“转化为、带来”；actual 强调实际发生的贸易增长。
  • excessive documentation：excessive 意为“过多的”；documentation 此处指文件和单证手续。

第 10 段 Paragraph 10

EN

Infrastructure alone is not sufficient for economic growth; other factors behind the border also need to be addressed. While transport costs are not always the primary constraint to increased trade, land-based connectivity often focuses on the development of disadvantaged regions or security considerations, with economic activities primarily concentrated in coastal areas.

中译

仅有基础设施不足以促进经济增长；还需要处理境内的其他因素。虽然运输成本并不总是制约贸易增长的主要因素，陆上互联互通通常侧重于欠发达地区的发展或安全方面的考量，而经济活动则主要集中在沿海地区。

词句讲解
  • not sufficient for economic growth：sufficient for 表示“足以满足或实现……”；not sufficient 表示“并非充分条件”。
  • other factors behind the border：behind-the-border factors 指边境线以内影响贸易与经济活动的因素，可包括国内制度、监管及市场条件；文中未逐一列明。
  • with economic activities primarily concentrated：with + 名词 + 过去分词短语补充背景；concentrated 意为“集中于”。

填补融资缺口Closing the Financing Gap

第 11 段 Paragraph 11

EN

Closing the financing gap for Belt and Road projects, which require significant investment, can be achieved through a combination of public and private financing sources.

中译

要填补“一带一路”项目的融资缺口——这些项目需要大量投资——可以结合公共和私人融资来源。

词句讲解
  • Closing the financing gap：closing 是动名词，意为“填补”；financing gap 指融资需求与可获得资金之间的缺口。
  • which require significant investment：which 引导非限制性定语从句，补充说明 Belt and Road projects 需要大量投资。
  • a combination of public and private financing sources：a combination of 表示“……的结合”；public and private 指公共部门和私人部门的融资来源。

第 12 段 Paragraph 12

EN

Domestic finance—local bond markets. Domestic bond markets along the routes can be boosted by the Initiative, attracting global investors and expanding long-term capital markets. However, improving market infrastructure and harmonizing regulations are necessary.

中译

国内融资——当地债券市场。该倡议可以推动沿线国家的国内债券市场发展，吸引全球投资者，并扩大长期资本市场。不过，改善市场基础设施和协调监管规则是必要的。

词句讲解
  • Domestic finance—local bond markets：domestic finance 指国内融资；破折号后的 local bond markets 说明本段讨论的具体渠道。
  • attracting global investors：现在分词短语补充说明推动债券市场发展的结果或伴随作用；attract 指吸引。
  • harmonizing regulations：harmonize 表示“使协调一致”；此处指协调不同地区的监管规则。

第 13 段 Paragraph 13

EN

Commercial finance. Commercial banks are suitable for revenue-generating projects, but public-private partnerships (PPPs) are needed for lower return projects. Corporate and project bonds face constraints due to immature domestic capital markets.

中译

商业融资。商业银行适合为能够产生收益的项目提供融资，但回报较低的项目需要公私合作（PPP）模式。由于国内资本市场尚不成熟，企业债券和项目债券面临限制。

词句讲解
  • revenue-generating projects：revenue-generating 是复合形容词，意为“能够产生收入的”；revenue 指收入。
  • public-private partnerships (PPPs)：指公共部门与私人部门合作的模式，PPPs 是 public-private partnerships 的缩写。
  • face constraints due to immature domestic capital markets：face constraints 意为“面临限制”；due to 引出原因，immature 在此指市场尚不成熟。

第 14 段 Paragraph 14

EN

Development banks. Development banks, such as the ADB and World Bank, play a crucial role in infrastructure financing and risk mitigation. Co-financing among national and international development banks can diversify risk.

中译

开发性银行。亚洲开发银行和世界银行等开发性银行在基础设施融资和缓释风险方面发挥重要作用。国家级和国际开发性银行之间的联合融资可以分散风险。

词句讲解
  • risk mitigation：mitigation 指减轻或缓和；risk mitigation 在金融语境中意为“风险缓释”。
  • Co-financing：co- 表示“共同”，co-financing 指两个或多个融资方共同提供资金。
  • diversify risk：diversify 在金融语境中表示“分散”；diversify risk 指通过多方或多项目分散风险。

第 15 段 Paragraph 15

EN

Silk Road Fund. The new Silk Road Fund has a capital base of $40 billion with the major aim of promoting private investment of Chinese enterprises abroad. The fund is sponsored by China's foreign exchange reserves, as well as the government and state-owned financial institutions. The Silk Road Fund promotes private investment abroad, while the Initiative can stimulate capital market development in Asia.

中译

丝路基金。新设立的丝路基金资本金为400亿美元，主要目标是促进中国企业的海外私人投资。该基金由中国外汇储备以及政府和国有金融机构出资支持。丝路基金促进海外私人投资，而“一带一路”倡议能够推动亚洲资本市场的发展。

词句讲解
  • a capital base of $40 billion：capital base 指资本基础或资本金；$40 billion 为400亿美元。
  • foreign exchange reserves：指外汇储备；foreign exchange 也可用于表示外汇或外汇交易。
  • while the Initiative can stimulate：while 在此连接两个相关但不同的作用，译为“而”；stimulate 表示“刺激、推动”。

第 16 段 Paragraph 16

EN

Opportunities for currency and bond markets in Asia and Europe. The large infrastructure investment envisaged in the BRI will probably have a significant impact on financial markets. The vast amounts of money needed will inject fresh momentum for capital market development in Asia, contributing to the intermediation of the considerable regional savings into investments in Asia.

中译

亚洲和欧洲外汇市场及债券市场面临的机遇。“一带一路”倡议所设想的大规模基础设施投资，可能对金融市场产生重大影响。所需的巨额资金将为亚洲资本市场发展注入新的动力，有助于通过金融中介将该地区相当可观的储蓄转化为在亚洲的投资。

词句讲解
  • envisaged in the BRI：envisaged 为过去分词，后置修饰 investment，意为“在‘一带一路’倡议中设想的”。
  • inject fresh momentum：inject 意为“注入”；momentum 在此指发展的动力或势头。
  • the intermediation of the considerable regional savings into investments：intermediation 指金融中介将储蓄引向投资的过程；considerable 意为“相当大的、可观的”。

第 17 段 Paragraph 17

EN

Challenges to scaling up infrastructure finance. A major issue for the scaling-up of financing for infrastructure and for industrial projects in the framework of the Belt and Road Initiative is the limits to debt financing, particularly financing in foreign currencies. Another issue is the high development costs for projects. The large number of additional shovel-ready projects in the regions of the Belt and Road to be financed in the short to medium term simply does not exist.

中译

扩大基础设施融资面临的挑战。在“一带一路”倡议框架下，扩大基础设施和工业项目融资的一个主要问题是债务融资所受的限制，尤其是外币融资方面的限制。另一个问题是项目开发成本高。事实上，沿线地区并不存在数量足够多、可在短中期内获得融资并立即开工的新增项目。

词句讲解
  • the limits to debt financing：limits to 表示“对……的限制”；debt financing 指通过借款或发行债务工具筹集资金。
  • shovel-ready projects：shovel-ready 是复合形容词，字面指“铲子已准备好”，实指各项前期准备完成、可以开工的项目。
  • to be financed in the short to medium term：不定式被动结构作后置定语，修饰 projects；in the short to medium term 指在短期至中期内。

跨境互联互通的治理：制度挑战Governing Cross-border Connectivity—the Institutional Challenge

第 18 段 Paragraph 18

EN

Countries along the Belt and Road face challenges due to diverse levels of development and dysfunctional governance, hindering infrastructure, trade and investment. A comprehensive policy approach is needed beyond focusing on individual projects, including support through policy advice, peer learning, development banks and common standards. To realize the Initiative's potential, platforms, partnerships and governance mechanisms must be established, involving both private business and government institutions. Multilateralizing the Initiative would enhance credibility, facilitate engagement and align investments with development aspirations. Supranational governance regimes and regional cooperation programs should inform the establishment of appropriate structures. Development banks should adopt regional planning and financing approaches, create innovative solutions to leverage private capital and coordinate efforts to exploit comparative advantages. Priority should be given to sustainability, cleaner transport, low-carbon urbanization, technology integration and cross-border value chains. The Belt and Road Initiative must raise its ambition to align with the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

中译

“一带一路”沿线国家的发展水平各不相同，治理机制运转不畅，由此面临挑战，并妨碍基础设施建设、贸易和投资。除关注单个项目之外，还需要采取全面的政策方针，包括通过政策建议、同伴学习、开发性银行和共同标准提供支持。为了发挥这一倡议的潜力，必须建立平台、伙伴关系和治理机制，让私营企业与政府机构共同参与。推动该倡议多边化将有助于提高其公信力、促进各方参与，并使投资与发展愿景相协调。超国家治理机制和区域合作计划应为建立适当的组织架构提供借鉴。开发性银行应采用区域规划和融资方法，设计创新方案以撬动私人资本，并协调各方努力，发挥各自的比较优势。应优先考虑可持续性、更清洁的交通、低碳城市化、技术融合和跨境价值链。“一带一路”倡议必须提高目标，与《2030年可持续发展议程》保持一致。

词句讲解
  • dysfunctional governance：dysfunctional 意为“功能失调、运转不畅的”；governance 指治理，此处说的是治理机制的运作问题。
  • peer learning：peer learning 指同伴之间交流经验、相互学习；peer 在此指处于相近地位的个人或机构。
  • align investments with development aspirations：align A with B 表示“使 A 与 B 协调一致”；aspirations 指愿望、目标或愿景。
  • leverage private capital：leverage 作动词时表示“利用、撬动”；此处指通过相关安排带动私人资本投入。
