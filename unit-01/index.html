第 1 段 Paragraph 1

EN

Today's world is facing escalating challenges, which contemporary forms of global governance are struggling to address. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept is worth considering in this scenario, as it can be taken not only as a tool for diplomacy but also as a profound philosophical notion which has a timeless impact. On the one hand, it carefully considers all the challenging questions being faced by the modern community; on the other hand, it also proposes deliberate answers to these challenges.

中译

当今世界面临着日益严峻的挑战，现有的全球治理方式在应对这些挑战时显得力不从心。在这一背景下，人类命运共同体理念值得认真思考，因为它既可以被视为一种外交工具，也可以被视为一种具有恒久影响的深刻哲学观念。一方面，它认真审视现代社会所面临的种种难题；另一方面，它也针对这些挑战提出了经过深思熟虑的应对之道。

词句讲解
  • escalating challenges：escalating 是现在分词作定语，表示“不断加剧的”；escalate 可用作不及物动词，也可表示“使升级”。
  • which contemporary forms of global governance are struggling to address：which 引导非限制性定语从句，指前面的 challenges；struggle to do 表示“费力地做某事”，address 在此意为“应对”，不是“地址”。
  • not only as a tool for diplomacy but also as a profound philosophical notion：not only ... but also ... 连接两个并列的 as 短语：“不仅作为……，也作为……”。profound 指“深刻的”，notion 指“观念”。

第 2 段 Paragraph 2

EN

The principles of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind share some similarities with the philosophical thought of Mencius. The intersection of the two philosophical beliefs is on the issues of governance and the application of virtue. Mencius lived in the Warring States Period (475–221 BC). His primary concern was the way leaders governed, which is reflected in his works, as it revolved around the issues of governance. Similarly, the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind is primarily concerned with improving global governance to create a better future for humanity.

中译

人类命运共同体的原则与孟子的哲学思想有一些相似之处。这两种哲学观念的交汇点，在于治理问题以及如何将德性付诸实践。孟子生活在战国时期（公元前475—前221年）。他主要关心的是领导者如何治理国家；这一点体现在他的著作中，因为其思想始终围绕治理问题展开。同样，人类命运共同体主要关注的是改善全球治理，为全人类创造更加美好的未来。

词句讲解
  • share some similarities with：share similarities with 表示“与……有相似之处”；some 限定相似的程度，不能译成“完全一致”。
  • The intersection of the two philosophical beliefs：intersection 本义为“交点、交集”；此处比喻两种思想的交汇点。
  • is primarily concerned with improving global governance：be concerned with 表示“关注、涉及”，with 是介词，后接动名词 improving。primarily 表示“主要地”。

孟子的哲学学说与人类命运共同体理念Mencius' Philosophical Teachings and the Concept of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind

第 3 段 Paragraph 3

EN

Mencius' theory states that all humans tend to do what is good. According to him, human beings have a natural affinity for Confucian virtues—benevolence (Ren), righteousness (Yi), propriety (Li), wisdom (Zhi) and fidelity (Xin). Mencius argues that this nature can be developed through a knowledge-based learning process coupled with self-discipline. For example, compassion is a subset of benevolence. According to Mencius, humans are not born with a fully developed virtuous inclination. Mencius posits that humans develop their humanity through enlarging and filling their hearts with benevolent inclinations. By filling the heart with benevolent inclinations, individuals will have unlimited reservoirs for doing good. An individual who has realigned his/her cognitive self and behavioral conduct towards benevolence will extend this inclination towards all other applicable paradigms. This is through the concept of extension, which helps an individual to see the similarity in situations, and extrapolates humaneness.

中译

孟子的理论认为，所有人都有向善的倾向。在他看来，人天生就亲近儒家所推崇的德性——仁、义、礼、智、信。孟子主张，通过以知识为基础的学习过程，并辅以自我约束，可以培养这种天性。例如，同情心便是仁爱的一种表现。按照孟子的观点，人并不是生来就具有充分发展的德性倾向。孟子提出，人们通过扩充内心、使其充满仁爱的倾向来发展自身的人性。当内心充满仁爱时，个人便拥有了取之不尽的行善源泉。一个人在认知和行为上都转向仁爱之后，会将这种倾向推及其他一切适用的情境。这便是“扩充”的含义：它帮助人们看见不同情境之间的相似之处，并将仁爱之心推而广之。

词句讲解
  • have a natural affinity for Confucian virtues：affinity for 表示“对……有亲近感或天然倾向”；virtues 在此是“德性”，不是“优点”这一泛义。
  • a knowledge-based learning process coupled with self-discipline：knowledge-based 是复合形容词，作 learning process 的定语；coupled with 相当于“与……结合”，此处译为“辅以自律”。
  • An individual who has realigned his/her cognitive self and behavioral conduct towards benevolence：who 引导定语从句修饰 individual；realign ... towards 意为“使……重新转向”，cognitive 指认知方面的。
  • extrapolates humaneness：extrapolate 原指根据已知信息外推，此处指将 humaneness（仁爱之心）推广到相似情境。

第 4 段 Paragraph 4

EN

The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept shares some similarities with Mencius' teachings, as both philosophies point to conscious-based solution-seeking. In Mencius' teachings, all people must continually pursue more knowledge so that they will attain benevolent qualities that activate their natural, innate tendency to do good, while the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept advocates multilateral engagement. Such engagements are related to Mencius' teachings as they are also based on the understanding of the inherent mutual benefits to be accrued by the participant nations. Multilateral engagements lead to reciprocal benefits, collaborative solutions and benevolent relationships between nations, for the greater good of the citizenry of those nations. Bilateral and multilateral engagements should be carefully drafted to ensure that they lead to a net benefit for all the parties that are involved. Mencius also identified the extension of knowledge as another way of building the capacity for goodness. The extension of knowledge and thought processes, as perceived by Mencius, has its equivalent in the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept, which is marked by international engagements and dialogues to find solutions to world problems. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept advocates the use of international organizations, such as the United Nations, to address the challenges that the world is facing. The United Nations and other international organizations employ research-based knowledge to gain an understanding of world challenges before they apply certain mechanisms to dealing with such challenges.

中译

人类命运共同体理念与孟子的学说有一些相似之处，因为二者都主张以自觉意识为基础寻求解决办法。在孟子的学说中，所有人都必须不断求知，以获得仁爱的品质，激发自身天生、内在的向善倾向；而人类命运共同体理念则倡导多边交往。这种交往与孟子的学说相通，因为它同样建立在对参与国之间内在互利关系的认识之上。多边交往能够带来互惠利益、协作解决方案以及国家间的友善关系，从而增进这些国家人民的整体福祉。双边和多边交往的安排应经过审慎设计，以确保所有参与方都能获得净收益。孟子还将知识的扩充视为增强行善能力的另一种途径。孟子所理解的知识与思维过程的扩展，在人类命运共同体理念中也有相应体现：各国通过国际交往和对话，寻找解决世界性问题的办法。人类命运共同体理念主张借助联合国等国际组织，应对世界面临的挑战。联合国和其他国际组织先利用研究所得的知识认识世界性挑战，再运用一定的机制处理这些挑战。

词句讲解
  • so that they will attain benevolent qualities that activate their natural, innate tendency to do good：so that 引出目的；后一个 that 引导定语从句修饰 qualities。benevolent 意为“仁爱的”，innate tendency 意为“先天倾向”。
  • the inherent mutual benefits to be accrued by the participant nations：inherent 意为“内在的”，mutual benefits 指“共同利益”；to be accrued 在原句中作后置定语，表达参与国将获得的利益。
  • Bilateral and multilateral engagements：bilateral 意为“双边的”，multilateral 意为“多边的”；engagements 在本文指交往或合作。
  • ensure that they lead to a net benefit for all the parties that are involved：ensure that 意为“确保……”；net benefit 是“净收益”，that are involved 修饰 parties，指所有参与方。

第 5 段 Paragraph 5

EN

Another philosophical similarity between the philosophy of Mencius and the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept is that they both advocate deliberate efforts to ameliorate certain states of affairs. According to Mencius, the conditions necessary for the extension of humanity in society include having ethical knowledge, making individual efforts and ensuring the availability of basic needs. Mencius' logic is that if people are well-fed and comfortable but lack proper knowledge, they will be no better than animals. To achieve the idealized community of humanity, considerable efforts are required to overcome moral weakness in times of adversity. Similarly, the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept advocates for the world to push back against nations that seek to exploit other nations and those that seek world dominance. Both frameworks insist that the ideal community of humanity cannot be achieved naturally; rather, it requires sustained efforts.

中译

孟子哲学与人类命运共同体理念的另一项哲学共通之处，是二者都倡导有意识地努力改善现状。按照孟子的观点，在社会中扩充人道精神，需要具备道德知识、付出个人努力，并确保基本生活需要得到满足。孟子的逻辑是：如果人们吃得饱、生活安逸，却缺乏应有的知识，那么他们与禽兽便没有什么区别。要实现理想的人道共同体，就必须付出相当大的努力，克服逆境中的道德弱点。同样，人类命运共同体理念倡导世界各国抵制那些企图剥削他国、谋求世界霸权的国家。两种思想框架都强调，理想的人道共同体不会自然而然地形成，而是需要持续的努力。

词句讲解
  • deliberate efforts to ameliorate certain states of affairs：deliberate 在此作形容词，表示“有意识的、审慎的”；ameliorate 是“改善”，states of affairs 指“状况”。
  • the conditions necessary for the extension of humanity in society：necessary for ... 后置修饰 conditions；extension 在此指“扩充、推及”，而非电话分机或延期。
  • overcome moral weakness in times of adversity：in times of adversity 表示“在逆境中”；overcome weakness 表示“克服弱点”。
  • cannot be achieved naturally; rather, it requires sustained efforts：rather 在分号后引出更准确或相反的说法，意为“相反”；sustained efforts 指“持续不断的努力”。

孟子的仁爱原则与人类命运共同体理念Mencius' Humane Principles and the Concept of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind

第 6 段 Paragraph 6

EN

Mencius' humane principles intersect with the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept through his theory of human nature. The theory asserts that all people have an innate natural drive to act virtuously and to do good. Mencius once said, “All human beings have a mind that cannot bear to see the suffering of others.” He affirmed this by giving the analogy of a child that falls into a well. He reasoned that everyone would express his/her shock and concern, no matter who he/she was. As such, compassion and pity are two of the principal attributes of Mencius’ humaneness. Thus, Mencius' humaneness is rooted in moral behavior that one adapts.

中译

孟子的仁爱原则通过其人性理论，与人类命运共同体理念形成了交汇。这一理论认为，所有人都具有践行德性、行善的先天动力。孟子曾说：“人皆有不忍人之心。”他以一个孩子掉进井里的比喻来说明这一点。他认为，无论是谁，都会表现出惊惧与关切。因此，同情与怜悯是孟子仁爱思想的两个主要特征。由此可见，孟子的仁爱观根植于个人所采取的道德行为。

词句讲解
  • an innate natural drive to act virtuously：innate 是“先天的”，drive 指“动力”；不定式 to act virtuously 后置说明这一动力的内容。
  • a mind that cannot bear to see the suffering of others：that 引导定语从句修饰 mind；cannot bear to do 表示“不忍心做某事”，suffering 在此为名词“苦难”。
  • giving the analogy of a child that falls into a well：analogy 意为“类比”；that falls into a well 修饰 a child。介词 by 加动名词 giving 表示说明方式。

第 7 段 Paragraph 7

EN

Similarly, the concept that the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind relies on is anchored in ethical principles: justice, inclusivity and respect. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind highlights the need to tackle global problems, such as hunger, disease and poverty. Tackling these issues would alleviate the suffering of millions of people around the globe and correspond to Mencius' humaneness. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind advocates justice through international organizations such as the United Nations, and this advocacy requires evaluating issues from a humane perspective.

中译

同样，人类命运共同体所依托的理念，也扎根于正义、包容和尊重等伦理原则。人类命运共同体强调，需要解决饥饿、疾病和贫困等全球性问题。处理这些问题，能够减轻全球数以百万计民众的苦难，这与孟子的仁爱思想相呼应。人类命运共同体倡导通过联合国等国际组织维护正义，而这一主张要求人们从仁爱与人道的视角评价问题。

词句讲解
  • is anchored in ethical principles：be anchored in 字面为“锚定于”，此处引申为“扎根于、以……为基础”；ethical 表示“伦理的、道德的”。
  • Tackling these issues would alleviate the suffering：Tackling these issues 是动名词短语作主语；alleviate 意为“减轻、缓解”，would 表示此处所设想的结果。
  • evaluating issues from a humane perspective：evaluate ... from a ... perspective 意为“从……的视角评估……”；humane 指“仁爱的、人道的”，注意区分 human。

第 8 段 Paragraph 8

EN

The other point of correspondence between Mencius' humaneness and the concept of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind lies in the aspect of self-cultivation. Mencius says that if individuals cultivate humaneness and virtues to overcome moral weakness, then over time, they will become sages and overflow with humaneness. Mencius also says that human behavior is inconsistent due to conflicting volition which may hamper virtuous conduct. If people fill their hearts and continuously cultivate virtue, they will consciously act humanely. Similarly, prosperity in the world is sporadic and should be shared in a world that is facing new challenges day in, day out. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept seeks to ensure the even distribution of humaneness through institutions such as the United Nations, which safeguards and applies humaneness across the world without selectivity. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept insists that there should be continuous dialogues and inquiries to define the rules of engagement between nations, in an international organization, at both multilateral and bilateral levels. Mencius' concept of humaneness leads towards the cultivation of virtue, while the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept leads towards continuous dialogues, making the two concepts congruous.

中译

孟子的仁爱思想与人类命运共同体理念的另一个契合点，在于自我修养。孟子说，如果个人通过培育仁爱与德性来克服道德弱点，那么随着时间的推移，他们就会成为圣贤，内心充盈仁爱。孟子还指出，相互冲突的意志可能妨碍有德性的行为，因此人的行为会表现得不一致。如果人们不断充实内心、修养德性，就会自觉地以仁爱待人。同样，世界各地的繁荣分布并不均衡，而在一个每天都面临新挑战的世界中，繁荣应当为人们所共享。人类命运共同体理念力图通过联合国等机构，确保仁爱得到普遍而均等的施行；这些机构在全世界维护并践行仁爱，不作选择性的区别对待。人类命运共同体理念强调，应在国际组织中持续开展对话与探讨，在多边和双边两个层面明确国家间交往的规则。孟子的仁爱观导向德性的培育，人类命运共同体理念则导向持续的对话，因而二者具有相通之处。

词句讲解
  • point of correspondence between：correspondence between ... and ... 在此表示“……与……之间的对应或契合”，不是“书信往来”。
  • conflicting volition which may hamper virtuous conduct：volition 意为“意志”，hamper 为“妨碍”；which 引导定语从句，说明相互冲突的意志可能造成的影响。
  • prosperity in the world is sporadic：prosperity 是“繁荣”，sporadic 是“零星的、不连续的”；结合上下文可理解为繁荣并非处处都有。
  • at both multilateral and bilateral levels：at ... levels 表示“在……层面”；multilateral 和 bilateral 分别对应“多边”和“双边”。

孟子的治理伦理与人类命运共同体理念Mencius' Ethics of Governance and the Concept of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind

第 9 段 Paragraph 9

EN

Mencius asserts that the state has a mandate to provide security for its citizenry, meet their basic needs and ensure that the subjects have faith in their leadership. Mencius claims that, to achieve these goals, nations must avoid unjustified wars, institute programs that cater to the economic welfare of their people and ensure the fair dispensation. According to Mencius, actions should not be judged on whether they will bring any benefit to an individual or a nation, but rather on the merit of whether they are morally upright or not upright. Mencius identifies that the external structure of the government has to be built on the use of good laws, the implementation of wise policies and rituals that reinforce humanity in leadership. The internal structures are constituents of the inner motivations of the rules, and, according to Mencius, the supreme motivations should be guided by benevolence and righteousness.

中译

孟子主张，国家有责任保障民众的安全，满足他们的基本需要，并确保民众信任自己的领导者。孟子认为，要实现这些目标，各国必须避免不义之战，推行照顾民众经济福祉的举措，并确保公正分配。按照孟子的观点，评判一种行为，不应看它能否给个人或国家带来利益，而应看它在道德上是否正当。孟子指出，政府的外在结构必须建立在良法的运用、明智政策的实施以及能够强化领导者仁爱精神的礼制之上。其内在结构则是统治者内心动机的组成部分；在孟子看来，最高的动机应当以仁义为指引。

校读注：原文印为“the inner motivations of the rules”，译文按上下文理解为“统治者的内心动机”；rules 可能是 rulers 的笔误。

词句讲解
  • has a mandate to provide security for its citizenry：mandate 在此是“职责、使命”；citizenry 是集合名词，指“全体公民”。to provide 等不定式说明职责内容。
  • not be judged on whether they will bring any benefit：not ... but rather ... 构成“不是根据……而是根据……”的对照；两个 whether 从句分别说明判断标准。
  • the inner motivations of the rules：rules 是原书可见用词；结合上下文疑似想写 rulers（统治者）。译文按后者理解，不能把这种推测当成原文的确定修订。

第 10 段 Paragraph 10

EN

The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept is related to these ethics of governance, as they both advocate the cessation of hostilities, the use of alternative approaches to the resolution of conflicts and the advancement of humanity. The underpinning of the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept, in this regard, can be traced back to the 2011 White Paper, which argues that the international community should reject cold-war and real-war mentalities. Furthermore, the 2011 White Paper says that countries need to find ways of engagement that ensure mutual benefit. This postulation is the equivalent of Mencius proposition that governing bodies have a mandate to provide for their citizens. Therefore, the two principles underpin the need to advance humanity by the government.

中译

人类命运共同体理念与这些治理伦理相联系，因为二者都倡导停止敌对行动，以其他途径解决冲突，并推动人道进步。就这一点而言，人类命运共同体理念的思想基础，可以追溯到2011年的白皮书；该白皮书主张，国际社会应摒弃冷战思维和实际战争思维。此外，2011年的白皮书指出，各国需要寻找能够确保互利的交往方式。这一主张与孟子关于治理机构有责任供养民众、保障民生的观点相对应。因此，这两项原则都强调，政府有必要推动人道进步。

词句讲解
  • the cessation of hostilities：cessation 意为“停止、终止”；hostilities 是 hostility 的复数，此处指“敌对行动”。
  • can be traced back to the 2011 White Paper：be traced back to 意为“可追溯到……”，which 引导非限制性定语从句，补充说明这份白皮书。
  • This postulation is the equivalent of：postulation 指“提出的主张、假定”；the equivalent of 在此意为“与……相对应的事物”，不必强译为完全等同。

第 11 段 Paragraph 11

EN

Another similarity between the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept and Mencius' teachings is that both advocate governance that tends towards socialism. Mencius advocates the provision of basic needs for the people by their rulers and the division of labor, an element of socialism. Further, Mencius advocates communal land divisions and the assignment of communal responsibilities. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept advocates global governance which is guided by shared values and mandates. One example is the Paris Agreement, which is aimed at conservation. This accord places the responsibility on countries to reduce their carbon emissions.

中译

人类命运共同体理念与孟子学说的另一项相似之处，是二者都倡导趋向社会主义的治理方式。孟子主张，由统治者满足人民的基本需要，并实行劳动分工，而劳动分工是社会主义的一个要素。此外，孟子还倡导共同体的土地划分以及公共责任的分派。人类命运共同体理念倡导以共同价值和共同职责为指引的全球治理。一个例子是旨在保护环境的《巴黎协定》。这一协定要求各国承担减少碳排放的责任。

词句讲解
  • the provision of basic needs for the people by their rulers：provision of ... for ... 表示“向……提供……”；by their rulers 表示提供者，整个名词短语相当于“统治者满足人民的基本需要”。
  • communal land divisions and the assignment of communal responsibilities：communal 指“共同体的、公共的”；assignment 是“分派”，此处指土地划分及公共责任的分配。
  • which is aimed at conservation：which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 the Paris Agreement；be aimed at 意为“旨在”，conservation 此处为“环境保护”。

第 12 段 Paragraph 12

EN

After a close evaluation of such accords, it is evident that they are based on socialist ideals of shared responsibility. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept also promotes joint activities, such as collaborative space exploration instead of the space race, which also echoes socialism.

中译

仔细考察这类协定，可以看出，它们建立在共同承担责任的社会主义理想之上。人类命运共同体理念还倡导联合行动，例如开展合作性的太空探索，而不是进行太空竞赛；这也呼应了社会主义理念。

词句讲解
  • After a close evaluation of such accords：close evaluation 表示“仔细评估”，accord 在此是“协议、协定”，不是动词“给予”。
  • it is evident that they are based on：it 是形式主语，that 从句是真正的主语；it is evident that ... 意为“显然……”。
  • collaborative space exploration instead of the space race：collaborative 意为“合作的”，exploration 为“探索”；instead of 表示“而非”，后接名词短语。

第 13 段 Paragraph 13

EN

To conclude, Mencius' humane principles are applied by the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept, as it advocates developed nations to help developing nations. The Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept advocates continuous dialogues, just as Mencius' tenet with righteousness advocates continuous knowledge acquisition. In addition, Mencius’ governance philosophy is congruent with the Community with a Shared Future for Mankind concept's insistence on responsible governance.

中译

总之，人类命运共同体理念倡导发达国家帮助发展中国家，从而将孟子的仁爱原则付诸实践。人类命运共同体理念倡导持续对话，正如孟子以义为核心的主张倡导不断获取知识一样。此外，孟子的治理哲学，也与人类命运共同体理念对负责任治理的坚持相契合。

词句讲解
  • as it advocates developed nations to help developing nations：as 在此引出原因；developed nations 与 developing nations 分别指“发达国家”和“发展中国家”。原文 advocate sb to do 用法不够地道，更常见的是 advocate doing 或 advocate that ...
  • just as Mencius' tenet with righteousness advocates continuous knowledge acquisition：just as 在此表示“正如”；tenet 是“原则、信条”，acquisition 是“获取”。原句 tenet with righteousness 表达较别扭，译文按上下文理解。
  • is congruent with：be congruent with 表示“与……一致、相符”；congruent 可与第8段的 congruous 一起辨认。
